🍕 Homemade Pizza

Crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, and whatever toppings you're craving — better than delivery, every time.

0 minPrep
0 minCook
0Serves
EasyDifficulty

📖 Description

Pizza originated in Italy and has become one of the most popular dishes around the world. Homemade pizza lets you customize your favorite toppings while enjoying a fresh, crispy crust and perfectly melted cheese. It's an excellent meal for family dinners, parties, or weekend treats.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 🍞 1 pizza dough
  • 🍅 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 🧀 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 🍖 Pepperoni slices
  • 🫒 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 🫑 Bell peppers
  • 🧅 Onion slices
  • 🍄 Mushrooms
  • 🌿 Italian seasoning
  • 🌱 Fresh basil leaves, optional

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C (392°F).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a baking tray.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese generously.
  5. Add pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, and bell peppers.
  6. Drizzle olive oil over the toppings.
  7. Sprinkle Italian seasoning for extra flavor.
  8. Bake for 15–20 minutes until the crust becomes golden brown.
  9. Remove from the oven and let it cool for 2–3 minutes.
  10. Slice into pieces and serve hot.

🍕 Fresh, cheesy, and delicious. Enjoy your homemade pizza!
