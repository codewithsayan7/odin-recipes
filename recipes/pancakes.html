🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

Soft, fluffy, and stacked high — the kind of breakfast that turns a slow morning into a good one.

0 minPrep
0 minCook
0Serves
EasyDifficulty

📖 Description

Pancakes are one of the world's most popular breakfast dishes. They are made using a simple batter of flour, eggs, milk, sugar, and baking powder. Their soft texture and golden color make them a favorite for both children and adults. Serve them warm with maple syrup, honey, fresh berries, bananas, or whipped cream for an unforgettable breakfast.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 🥣 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 🥛 1½ cups milk
  • 🥚 2 eggs
  • 🍬 2 tbsp sugar
  • 🧁 2 tsp baking powder
  • 🧈 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 🌿 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 🧂 A pinch of salt
  • 🍯 Maple syrup or honey
  • 🍓 Fresh fruits, optional

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Whisk together milk, eggs, vanilla extract, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until smooth.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered non-stick pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour about ¼ cup of batter onto the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface.
  7. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  8. Repeat for the remaining batter.
  9. Serve warm with maple syrup, honey, butter, or fresh fruits.

🥞 Enjoy your soft, fluffy homemade pancakes!
