🍝 Classic Lasagna

Experience the rich flavors of Italy with this delicious homemade lasagna layered with pasta, savory meat sauce, creamy cheeses, and aromatic herbs.

📖 Description

Lasagna is one of the most famous Italian comfort foods. It is prepared by layering pasta sheets with seasoned meat sauce, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, Parmesan cheese, and herbs. Baked until golden and bubbling, lasagna is perfect for family dinners, celebrations, or whenever you're craving a hearty meal.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 🍝 12 Lasagna noodles
  • 🥩 500g Ground beef
  • 🍅 2 cups Tomato sauce
  • 🧀 250g Ricotta cheese
  • 🧀 250g Mozzarella cheese
  • 🧀 50g Parmesan cheese
  • 🧄 2 Garlic cloves (minced)
  • 🫒 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 🌿 Italian seasoning
  • 🧂 Salt and black pepper

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Pour in the tomato sauce and season with Italian herbs, salt, and pepper.
  6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  7. Add a layer of noodles, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, and more sauce.
  8. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  9. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Bake for 35–40 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling.
  11. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
  12. Serve hot and enjoy!

🍝 Rich, cheesy, and full of flavor. Enjoy your homemade Classic Lasagna!
